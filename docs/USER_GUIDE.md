# User guide

Adroit Integrated Management Platform: Employee Management and Vehicle & Equipment Management in one system. You see only the modules, records and documents your role allows.

## Signing in

Open the system address in a browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox; phones work too). Sign in with your e-mail and password. The first time, or after a reset, you'll be asked to choose your own password: at least 10 characters with letters and a number. After 5 wrong attempts the account locks for 15 minutes. To change your password later, click the lock icon next to your name at the top right.

## Everyone

- **Management Home**: your key numbers, quick search, the AI box, and the items assigned to you.
- **Attention Centre**: every expired, urgent, due or missing document you're allowed to see, from both modules. Filter by module, company, department, location, document type, status, responsible officer and expiry range. Choose yourself as the responsible officer (or click the bell) to see what is assigned to you.
- **Search**: one box for employee number or name, fleet number, plate, chassis, and document or policy numbers.
- **AI Assistant**: ask in plain English, e.g. "Show visas expiring in the next 60 days". Answers list the records they came from and never change anything.
- **Notifications** (bell icon): alerts assigned to you. Click one to open the record.

### Document statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Expired | Past the expiry date |
| Urgent | Expires within the urgent window (default 30 days) |
| Renewal due | Expires within the warning window (default 60 days) |
| Monitor | Expires within the extended window (default 90 days) |
| Valid | Outside the warning windows |
| On file | No expiry (e.g. certificates) |
| No scan / Not recorded | The scanned copy or the record itself is missing |

### Renewal actions

When a document enters its warning window, a renewal action opens (**Open**) and is assigned to the responsible officer. Update it as work progresses (**In Progress**, **Submitted to authority**, **Awaiting payment**, **On hold**). When the new document arrives, use **Renew · record new document**: enter the new number and expiry, and attach the scan. The old version moves to the document's history and the action closes as **Completed**.

## HR Officer

- **Employees**: add, edit, and import from CSV. When adding an employee, enter the **employee number** (it must be unique and cannot be changed later), the **employee code** and the **Emp (MOL) ID**. Filter the list by department, company, sponsor, employee code, nationality and status. Each employee has one profile with tabs for Personal, Employment, Passport, Visa, Emirates ID, Labour Card, Insurance, Documents and Leave.
- **MOL Register**: two tables, Company Name → MOL Code and Employee Name → Emp (MOL) ID. HR records or corrects the codes in place; each code must be unique.
- **Dates** are shown and entered as dd/mm/yyyy everywhere. Type the date, or pick it with the calendar button in the field.
- **HR Documents & Expiry**: the expiry centre, what's assigned to you, the document library, and missing documents.
- **Leave & Rejoining**:
  - submit requests;
  - review requests from department heads (**Checked · send for approval**, or **Reject** with a reason);
  - record rejoining when employees return;
  - see who is away in the leave planner.
- **HR Reports**: the renewal workload for the next 12 months and department status.

## PRO / Compliance Officer

You see employees with their passports, visas and Emirates IDs. Renewal alerts for these arrive in your notifications, and the Attention Centre lists them when you filter by yourself as the responsible officer. Update each renewal action and record the renewed document with its scan.

## Insurance Officer

You see health insurance for employees and motor insurance for vehicles, in both modules. You renew and upload insurance documents.

## Department Head

You see the employees in your department, without dates of birth, contact and personal details, insurance or documents. Submit leave requests (**Leave & Rejoining → New leave request**) and record when an employee rejoins. HR reviews each request and Management approves it.

## Management

You see everything read-only, approve or reject leave requests after HR review, and receive escalations for expired documents and the weekly digest. **Audit log** shows every change.

## Fleet / Transport Officer

- **Fleet Master**: add, edit and import vehicles and machines. Each asset has one profile with tabs for Overview, Registration, Insurance, Safety, Certificates, Documents and History. Mark sold or scrapped assets **Inactive** or **Disposed**; their history stays and alerts stop.
- **Fleet Documents & Expiry**: the same tools as HR, for registration, insurance, safety, inspection and permits.
- **Fleet Reports**: the renewal workload and compliance by category, company and location.

## Read-Only Auditor

You see all records and the audit log, without being able to change anything.

## Uploading files

Scans must be PDF, JPG or PNG (the administrator can allow others), up to the size limit shown on the upload form (default 10 MB). The system checks the file's content, so a renamed file of another type is refused.
