## 1. Executive Summary

*A practical HR modernization project focused on the work currently handled manually.*

### Current Position
- Standalone HR application on one computer
- Physical files and scanned documents handled separately
- Manual monitoring of Visa, Emirates ID, Passport and Insurance expiries
- Manual leave follow-up and rejoining records
- Repeated telephone / email / verbal follow-up between responsible officers

### Proposed Position
- Web-based HR system accessible to authorized users
- One central employee profile with digital documents
- Automatic expiry monitoring and designated-officer alerts
- Professional online leave workflow from request to rejoining
- AI-assisted search, summaries and attention lists

> Phase 1 deliberately excludes salary processing. The existing payroll/WPS arrangement remains outside this proposal.

## 3. Proposed Phase 1 System

*A single online HR workspace for employee information, documents, alerts and leave.*

| # | Module | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Employee Master** | One employee profile containing personal, employment, department, contact and sponsorship information. |
| 02 | **Document Centre** | Passport, Emirates ID, Visa, Insurance and other major scanned documents available from the employee profile. |
| 03 | **Expiry & Alert Centre** | Upcoming expiries automatically highlighted and routed to the designated responsible officer. |
| 04 | **Leave Management** | Online leave application, review, approval, vacation tracking and employee rejoining. |
| 05 | **Management Dashboard** | Immediate view of pending renewals, employees on leave, missing documents and matters requiring attention. |
| 06 | **AI Assistance** | Simple natural-language assistance to find records, documents, expiry lists and HR summaries quickly. |

> **Design principle:** ONE EMPLOYEE → ONE PROFILE → ALL HR INFORMATION & DOCUMENTS IN ONE PLACE

## 7. Professional Leave Management

*A complete digital record from leave request to employee rejoining.*

| Step | Stage | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Leave Request** | Department Head / authorized user submits request. |
| 2 | **HR Review** | HR checks eligibility, dates and employee record. |
| 3 | **Approval** | Approved / rejected status is recorded. |
| 4 | **Vacation** | Employee leave period is visible in the system. |
| 5 | **Rejoining** | Return-to-duty / rejoining record is completed. |
| 6 | **History** | Complete leave history remains available for future reference. |

- **Leave Types:** Annual Leave • Emergency Leave • Sick Leave
- **Management Visibility:** Who is on leave, upcoming leave, pending requests and expected return dates.
- **Paper Reduction:** Applications, approvals and rejoining records remain electronically linked to the employee.
